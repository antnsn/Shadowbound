## Why Shadowbound exists

A rogue keeps poison on both weapons. Each coat lasts 30 minutes or until its charges run out, and it is easy to fight a whole pull without it. Vanish needs Flash Powder and Blind needs Blinding Powder, and both run out at the wrong moment.

Shadowbound watches all of that. It shows a card when a weapon has no poison or its poison is about to run out, and you click the card to apply the poison from your bags. It warns you before your powders or your poisons run out. It only loads on rogues.

## What it does

- **Poison cards.** A card for each weapon when it has no poison or the poison runs out soon. Click it to apply the poison you picked for that hand, the best rank in your bags. [More](features/poisons.html)
- **Buff key.** Out of combat, each press applies the poison a card asks for. [More](features/buff-key.html)
- **Reagents.** A card when Flash Powder, Blinding Powder or the poisons you use run low. [More](features/reagents.html)
- **Travel panel.** Press Escape: your Hearthstone sits beside the game menu. [More](features/travel.html)
- **Well-Rested and Well Fed.** A warning and a countdown before they run out. [More](features/well-rested-and-well-fed.html)
- **Explanations.** Hover over a card or button to read what it does and why. [More](features/explanations.html)
- **Your layout.** Every frame can be moved, snapped to a grid and resized. [More](features/frames-and-minimap.html)
- **Three skins.** Shadow, Blizzard and Flat. [More](features/skins.html)

## The Bound family

Shadowbound is one of the Bound addons, one per class, built on the same core: Lightbound (paladin), Chronobound (mage), Stormbound (shaman), Packbound (hunter), Spiritbound (priest), Felbound (warlock), Dreambound (druid), Ragebound (warrior) and Shadowbound (rogue). They look like their class but work the same way, so if you know one, you know them all.
