# Explanations

Hover over a Shadowbound card or the travel panel's Hearthstone row and the tooltip says, in a sentence or two, what it is for. Turn it off once you know it all.

## What it does

The explanations show in the tooltips of:

- the two [poison cards](poisons.html), also when a card offers a click (the tooltip then names the poison, for example **Instant Poison IV**, and ends with **Click to cast (out of combat)**),
- the [reagents card](reagents.html),
- the [Well-Rested and Well Fed](well-rested-and-well-fed.html) cards,
- the Hearthstone row of the [travel panel](travel.html).

The texts:

| Where | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| Main-hand poison card | Poison on your main-hand weapon adds its effect to your hits. It lasts 30 minutes or until its charges are used up. |
| Off-hand poison card | Poison on your off-hand weapon adds its effect to your hits. It lasts 30 minutes or until its charges are used up. |
| Reagents card | Vanish uses one Flash Powder, sold by reagent vendors and poison vendors. Blind uses one Blinding Powder. Rogues make it from Fadeleaf with the Poisons skill. Each poison item coats one weapon once. Rogues make poisons with the Poisons skill; poison vendors sell the vials and reagents. |
| Well-Rested card | Sleeping in a Cozy Sleeping Bag gives Well-Rested: more experience for a while. |
| Well Fed card | Eating buff food gives Well Fed: better stats, and on Forever more experience too. |
| Hearthstone (travel panel) | Your Hearthstone takes you back to the inn you set it to. Talk to an innkeeper to change it. |

## How to use it

Hover over a card or a row. That's all.

## Settings

In `/shadow`, page **Skin**, section **General**:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explain things in tooltips | On | Shows the explanations. Off: no explanations; a card that offers a click still shows the poison name and "Click to cast", and the Hearthstone row still shows its name. |

`/shadow explain` turns it on or off from chat.

## Good to know

**No tooltip while frames are unlocked.**
While you move frames, the cards don't show their explanations, so they don't get in the way.
