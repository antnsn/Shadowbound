# Combo points

Your finishers get stronger with each combo point. Shadowbound shows them as five drops, so you see at a glance when it is time for Eviscerate, Slice and Dice or Rupture.

## What it does

- **Five drops.** One lights up, with a green glow round it, for each combo point on your target. The rest are grey.
- **When it shows.** While you have combo points, and the whole time you are in combat. Out of combat with no points, it hides. Turn on **Always show the bar** to keep it up.
- **Pulse at 5.** When you reach 5 points, the drops flash a few times: time for a finisher. They flash once each time you reach 5, not again while you stay there.
- **Your size and place.** Unlock the frames to drag the bar where you want it, and scroll on it to make it bigger or smaller.

![The combo point bar with three points]({{ '/screenshots/frames/cppoint.png' | relative_url }})

## How to use it

1. Nothing to set up: the bar is on by default, below the middle of the screen.
2. Want it somewhere else? Click **Unlock frames** (or type `/shadow unlock`), drag the bar, then lock the frames again.

## Settings

In `/shadow`, page **Combo points**:

![The Combo points page]({{ '/screenshots/options-combo-points.png' | relative_url }})

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show the combo point bar | On | Turns the bar on or off. |
| Flash at 5 points | On | The drops flash a few times when you reach 5 points. |
| Always show the bar | Off | Keeps the bar up out of combat with no points too. |

## Good to know

**The bar stays hidden in combat.**
WoW: Forever hides some combat values from addons. If it hides combo points, Shadowbound hides the bar rather than show a wrong count. Tell us if that happens to you.

**The Blizzard and Flat skins.**
The painted drops belong to the Shadow skin. The Blizzard and Flat skins show five plain squares instead, lit in the same colour.
