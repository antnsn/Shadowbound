# Travel panel

Press Escape and a small **Travel** panel opens beside the game menu, with your Hearthstone. Click it to use it.

## What it does

![The travel panel beside the game menu]({{ '/screenshots/travel.png' | relative_url }})

- The panel shows whenever the game menu opens, out of combat.
- Rogues have no travel spells, so the panel holds one row: **Hearthstone**, with the inn it is set to, for example `Hearthstone: Lion's Pride Inn`.
- The row only shows while you carry a Hearthstone.
- Click the row to use your Hearthstone. The game menu closes after the click.

## How to use it

1. Press Escape.
2. Click **Hearthstone** in the panel.

## Settings

![The Travel page]({{ '/screenshots/options-travel.png' | relative_url }})

In `/shadow`, page **Travel**:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show the travel panel when the game menu opens | On | Turns the panel on or off. |

## Good to know

**Not in combat.**
The panel only opens out of combat, and it hides when a fight starts.

**Can I move the panel?**
No. It always sits to the right of the game menu.

**The panel is empty.**
You don't carry a Hearthstone. Get a new one from any innkeeper.
