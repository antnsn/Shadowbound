# Skins

Shadowbound comes in three looks. They all work the same way, only the frames look different, so you can match the rest of your UI.

## The three skins

| Skin | Looks like | Good with |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Shadow** (default) | Near-black panels with a purple edge and teal light, a drifting smoke layer, small blades in the corners and a faint crest behind each card. A card that needs you has a slow violet glow at its edge. | The default UI, or if you like the rogue look. |
| **Blizzard** | The game's own grey and silver tooltips, dialogs and buttons. No crest on the cards. | A mostly default UI. |
| **Flat** | Thin black edges on dark grey, Arial Narrow text, no crest. | EllesmereUI, ElvUI and other flat UI packs. |

## How to switch

![The Skin page]({{ '/screenshots/options-skin.png' | relative_url }})

1. Type `/shadow` and open the **Skin** page.
2. Click the **Skin** button to step through the skins (right-click goes back).
3. Click **Reload now**, or type `/reload` later. The new skin shows after the reload.

The skin is saved with your other Shadowbound settings.

## What changes, and what doesn't

A skin changes the frames: colors, borders, fonts, buttons and check boxes in the options window, the cards and the travel panel.

The warning color, for a weapon with no poison or a reagent at 0, stands out in every skin.

## Things to know

**Why does it need a reload?**
Shadowbound builds its frames once, when you log in. Switching in the middle of a session would leave some frames in the old look and some in the new one, so the switch waits for the next reload.
