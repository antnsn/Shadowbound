# Getting started
{: .no_toc }

From install to a rogue whose weapons never go unpoisoned unnoticed.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

1. TOC
{:toc}

---

## 1. Install it

Pick one:

- **CurseForge app or WowUp:** search for **Shadowbound** and click Install.
- **By hand:** download it from [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/shadowbound) or from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/antnsn/Shadowbound/releases) (the `Shadowbound-<version>.zip` file, not the green "Code" button). Unzip it into your `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns` folder, so you get a folder called `Shadowbound` there.

Start the game (or type `/reload` if it was already running) and log in on your rogue.

{: .note }
Shadowbound only loads on rogues. On any other character it turns itself off for that character from the next login, and `/shadow` only says that it is a rogue addon.

## 2. Open the options

Type `/shadow` in chat, or click the Shadowbound button on your minimap.

The options window has five pages on the left: **Poisons**, **Reagents**, **Travel**, **Extras** and **Skin**. Everything is on by default.

The default skin, Shadow, is near-black with a purple edge. See [Skins](features/skins.html).

## 3. Let the cards do their job

The poison cards stay hidden until you carry a poison. Rogues learn poisons at level 20.

Once you carry one, play as usual. When a weapon has no poison, or its poison has 3 minutes left, a card shows up. Click it out of combat and Shadowbound applies the poison from your bags. See [Poisons](features/poisons.html).

By default each hand uses **Auto**: Instant Poison on the main hand, Deadly Poison on the off hand. To pick a poison per hand, open the **Poisons** page.

## 4. Pick a buff key

Optional, but handy before a pull.

1. In `/shadow`, open the **Extras** page.
2. Under **Buff key**, click the button next to **Key** and press the key you want.
3. Out of combat, press it. Each press applies the poison a card asks for, main hand first.

More in [Buff key](features/buff-key.html).

## 5. Put the frames where you want them

1. Type `/shadow unlock`, Shift-click the minimap button, or click **Unlock frames** at the bottom of the options window.
2. Drag each frame where you want it. Scroll the mouse wheel over a frame to resize it.
3. Type `/shadow lock` to lock them.

`/shadow reset` puts every frame back where it started. More in [Frames and minimap](features/frames-and-minimap.html).

## That's it

Everything else works on its own. When you want to know what a card means or change how something behaves, look it up under [Features](features/).
